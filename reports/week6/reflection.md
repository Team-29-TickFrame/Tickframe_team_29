# Week 6 Reflection

## Context

Week 6 focused on Sprint 4 trial-release readiness, customer-facing documentation entry points, and transition-readiness evidence.

## What worked well

- Sprint 4 planning and scope became inspectable through milestones and roadmap updates.
- Repository entry points improved (`README.md`, `CONTRIBUTING.md`, `AGENTS.md`, `docs/customer-handover.md`).
- Week 6 customer meeting provided practical transition feedback and clarified optional polish areas.

## What was difficult

- Meeting/demo discussion quality depended on stable communication and clear artifact traceability.
- Some transition evidence remained conditional (independent customer use/deployment not fully confirmed in-session).
- Team had to balance feature polish with documentation and reporting deadlines.

## What we learned

- Transition readiness requires not only product features but also clear, navigable documentation.
- Public/private evidence boundaries must be enforced continuously during reporting work.
- Early issue-to-artifact traceability reduces final-week reporting stress.

## Action items for Week 7

- Apply practical follow-up polish from Week 6 meeting where feasible.
- Finalize transition confirmation evidence and handover-status statements.
- Keep Week 7 report focused on verifiable links and concrete outcomes.
